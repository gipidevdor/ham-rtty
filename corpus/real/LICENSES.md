# Real recordings: sources and licences

No real RTTY recording has yet been admitted. The inventory contains four
explicit placeholders, not licensed or measured fixtures. The inherited CW
recordings and their evidence have been removed from this RTTY distribution.

Required entries: DWD/weather 50 baud and 450 Hz shift (three independently
identified clips), plus amateur RTTY 45.45 baud and 170 Hz shift. Before admission,
verify redistribution and adaptation rights for each recording; retain source
URL, author, licence evidence/legal text, capture date, transformations, original
and derivative hashes, independent reference text and measured CER/budget.

The code's Artistic 2.0 licence grants no rights to third-party radio recordings.
Do not insert a downloaded or WebSDR capture whose licence is unclear.

## Source review, 2026-10-27

- [Commons RTTY.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:RTTY.ogg) has a
  public-domain dedication, but its description identifies gMFSK-generated
  audio. It cannot satisfy the off-air requirement.
- [benj500's RTTY.wav](https://freesound.org/people/benj500/sounds/369476/)
  is described as a DX394 shortwave reception under CC0. It is a candidate,
  not an admitted fixture: signal parameters, independent transcript and an
  exact reviewed derivative/hash have not been established here.
- [W6IWI RTTY audio](https://w6iwi.org/rtty/audio/) includes an explicitly
  described WebSDR reception. No per-file redistribution licence was verified
  in this review, so no recording from that page is distributed.

A licence-qualified source alone does not supply a measured transcript or
prove the requested baud/shift. Do not replace the four required slots with
synthetic audio or claim CER before independently annotating and measuring it.
